Frequently asked questions

The details, without the fine print.

Is my brand design free?

Creating and downloading a sample label concept is free. Final artwork and product availability will be confirmed before any commercial order.

How much could I earn?

At the proposed $8 wholesale price and $16 suggested retail price, gross profit would be $8 per jar before taxes, fees and other expenses. Retail sales are not guaranteed.

Can I order today?

Not yet. This is a concept-testing site. Production, final label compliance, ordering and fulfillment are still being prepared.
